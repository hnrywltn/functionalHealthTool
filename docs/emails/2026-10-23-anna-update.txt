1. Signing in

2. Your account menu
There's a small circle with a person icon at the top right of every page. Click it to find:

3. Payments
The Payments page (in that menu) is where you pay for Health Reference, $50 a month. You can either:
- Turn on auto-pay, so your card is charged automatically each month. You can turn it off anytime and keep access through the month you've paid for.
- Pay one month at a time, without saving a card. If you pay early, the new month is added on after the one you've already paid for, so you never lose days.
The page shows what you've paid through and a history of your payments.

If a payment is coming up or overdue, you'll see a small reminder banner at the top of the page. It's only a reminder. It never locks you out of anything.

4. Contacts
As you asked, Contacts is now in the main menu. Each contact has Name, Title, Department, Company, Phone, Fax, Email, URL, Address, Date Met - Referral, and Notes. Like everything else, contacts can be searched, tagged, and linked to other entries.
